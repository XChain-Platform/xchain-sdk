xchain-sdk dead-code sweep
==========================

Taken at 957d9490eb59f7a40ce13c66a9356733b5217100 with `node bin/reachability.js
--siblings <platform tree>`, then falsified candidate by candidate with
`command grep -r` across all 22 xchain-* checkouts plus the platform tooling
directory. The tool-layer search honours .ignore files and comes back falsely
empty, so every line below is from `command grep`.

VERDICT: NOTHING IS DELETED. Four files are unreachable from this repo's own
runtime closure; all four are held from outside it, and three of the four are
held by something no repo-local walk can see. That is the whole point of the
falsification step, and this repo is the case that proves it.

WHY A LIBRARY IS DIFFERENT. package.json's `files` publishes the entire src/
tree, so any module in it is loadable by a consumer as
`@dankest-llc/xchain-sdk/src/<path>`. A file nothing in this checkout requires
is dead to this checkout and is not thereby dead to npm. Two of the four
candidates are exactly that shape, and one of them is deep-imported by name in
the published documentation.

CANDIDATE 1: src/cosigner/server.js                          KEPT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A published deep-import entry point, named as such in the documentation repo:

  xchain-documentation/ai-agents/agent-wallets.md:123

It is the HTTP sidecar half of the co-signer interface and it is reached only
by consumers, never by this package's own main. Held also by three suites here
(coSignerServer, cosignerHardening3, cosignerEnvelope) and cross-referenced by
src/utils/safeCompare.js.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
